      Help and shortcuts

      How each tool works

      Select (V)
      Click a shape to select it. Drag on empty space to select several; Shift adds to the selection. Drag to move, drag a corner to resize, drag the top circle to rotate. Right-click (or press and hold on a touch screen) for more actions.
      Pen (P)
      Click to place points. Press and drag a point to bend the line into a curve. Click the first point to close the shape; Enter or double-click finishes, Esc cancels. Click the end of an open line to keep drawing from it.
      Pencil (B)
      Draw freely. Let go to finish; the line is tidied up automatically.
      Line (L)
      Drag to draw a straight line. Hold Shift to snap the angle.
      Rectangle (R), Oval (E)
      Drag to draw. Hold Shift for a square or circle, Alt for rounded corners on a rectangle.
      Fill (G)
      Click inside a shape to fill it with the current colour. Drag across several shapes to fill them all.
      Outline (K)
      Click a shape's edge to give it the current line style. Drag across several edges to change them all.
      Edit points (T)
      Drag a point or its round handles. Click a small diamond to add a point; double-click a point to delete it. F makes a point smooth or sharp; G opens or closes the line.
      Eraser (C)
      Click a shape to remove it, or drag across shapes to remove several.
      Pick a colour (I)
      Click a shape to copy its colour into the Area or Border colour box.
      Move the view (H)
      Drag to move around the artboard. In any tool you can hold Space instead. On a touch screen, pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers.
